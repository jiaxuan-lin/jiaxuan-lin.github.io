About me

Hello! My name is Jiaxuan, and aside from being a video game fanatic back in 6th grade, I actually started my interest in making 3D things, even running a tiny online business selling models and renders. A CAD class in my sophomore year of high school turned that into engineering, and I've been exploring technology ever since! Currently, I'm on MIT's Solar Electric Vehicle Team working on improving the efficiency and aerodynamics of our car Solstice. I'm also currently pursuing personal projects, such as in photography, fabrication, and developing a game with the help of AI agents! I'm excited to continue developing my skillset and explore what there is to engineering!
