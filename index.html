About me

I love cats, music, and technology, and turning ideas into things I can hold.

I'm a freshman at MIT planning to study Mechanical Engineering with a concentration in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (Course 2-A6), with a double minor in Finance (15-3) and Economics (14).

I especially enjoy 3D design and modeling, like working with Autodesk Inventor and 3D printers.

In my free time I take photos. Buying a camera was one of the best risky investments I've made. It goes almost everywhere with me, and I'm always looking for overlooked details and hidden compositions.

This site is where I share my work: design and manufacturing, photography, and other creative projects. Thank you for stopping by :)
