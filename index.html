About me

Hello, my name is Jiaxuan! Back in 6th grade, aside from being a video game fanatic, I first started making things in 3D. I even ran a tiny online business selling models and renders. A CAD class in my sophomore year of high school turned that interest into engineering, and I’ve been exploring technology ever since.

Right now I’m on MIT’s Solar Electric Vehicle's Aerodynamics Team, working on improving the structure and efficiency of our car Solstice. On my own time, I enjoy taking photos, crafting things, playing badminton, and I'm currently developing a game with the help of AI agents.

I’m excited to keep building my skills and to see how far engineering can take me!
